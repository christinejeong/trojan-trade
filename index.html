ONE CAMPUS. ONE COMMUNITY.

A better way to pass it on.

Trojan Trade is a peer-to-peer resale platform concept for current USC students to buy, sell, and rent secondhand textbooks, furniture, electronics, and everyday essentials.

01 · Find your fitExplore by category, search for an essential, or browse rentals for the things you only need for a little while.

02 · Connect on campusThe intended experience connects students to discuss condition, availability, and a convenient public pickup spot.

03 · Keep good things goingSpend less on what you need and earn from belongings you're ready to share or pass on.

This portfolio demo uses sample listings. Saved items and your demo listings stay in this browser when storage is available. Messages, payments, and student verification are not connected.
